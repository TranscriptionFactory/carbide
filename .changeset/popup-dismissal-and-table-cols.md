Editor popups now close when you click away from them. The block-insert menu closes on any click outside it and stops taking over Enter and Tab. The slash menu, link suggestions and query/base/tasks suggestions stay closed after Escape or an outside click until you type more. The table toolbar hides on Escape or an outside click until the selection moves. New table columns get a "Col N" header, and the toolbar's Table button seeds "Col 1–3".
