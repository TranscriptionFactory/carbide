Recents gained a date range picker and lost the Quarter preset. The sidebar's Recents header now offers All / Today / Week / Month plus a calendar button: pick two days and the list shows notes whose modified (or created, when that is the sort) timestamp falls inside the range, including both endpoints and nothing after them. Picking only a start day reads as "since that day". The chosen range is remembered across restarts.

Quarter is gone. Anyone whose saved setting was Quarter sees All on the next launch instead of an empty list.
