Callouts now survive PDF, HTML and EPUB export. A `> [!type] Title` block exports as a coloured box with the type's icon and title instead of a plain quote showing the literal `[!type]` marker. Aliases, unknown types, custom colours and nested callouts render as they do in the editor, and folded callouts export expanded. In PDFs the callout title stays on the same page as the first line of its body.
